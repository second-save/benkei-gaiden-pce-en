# Benkei Gaiden (PC Engine, 1989) — Plot Synopsis

This synopsis captures the complete story arc verified from `script/b11_dialogue.json`, `script/b0a_locations.json`, and `script/b11_ending_nar.json`. Translators and story reviewers should use it so foreshadowing, reveals, and stakes land cleanly without spoiling later turns prematurely.

## Premise & Opening (Tsukumo Village & Rinsho-ji)
- **Kijaku** is an orphan raised at **Rinsho-ji** in **Tsukumo Village** by High Priest **Hogen**. Left at the temple as a baby with only the **Birth Note** bearing the name `鬼若` (`Kijaku`), he grows up unaware of his lineage.
- Demons raid Tsukumo Village and abduct **O-Koto** (trusted to Hogen by outlaw chief **Saburo**, and cared for by villager **Yoichi**) to **Cape Tower**, hoping to use her as leverage to seize the **Spirit Gem**.
- Kijaku rescues O-Koto and defeats the Cape Tower fiend, whose dying words warn that **demonic blood** flows in Kijaku's veins.
- Hogen tells Kijaku the truth of the Birth Note and sends him forth with Hogen's own letter of introduction to seek **Kanaya Kichijiro** in the capital of **Rikuzen**.

## Act 1 — Allies & The Secret of Kijaku's Birth
- **Rikuzen & Kasumi Keep:** Kichijiro grants Kijaku the **Kanaya Seal**, aiding his travels at Kanaya shops. Meanwhile, **Sukegoro** steals the Spirit Gem from Saburo's **Kasumi Band** before returning it under pressure. At **Kasumi Keep**, Saburo reveals he is **Ise Saburo**, thought slain alongside Benkei and Yoshitsune at **Koromogawa**, and gives Kijaku the Spirit Gem so they can open the **Hidden Pagoda** and uncover Kijaku's origin.
- **Dewa Sanzan & Sayaka:** To break the Hidden Pagoda's seal, they travel past rockbound **Dewa** to **Dewa Sanzan**, recruiting the young mystic **Sayaka** (while her attendant **Seira** keeps watch and senses threats from the Demon Realm). Demons serve the **Overlord of Conquest**, guardian of the **Dark Demon Gate**, and deploy assassins like **Shura King**.
- **The Cursed Realm & Benkei:** Obtaining the **Gold Blade** guarded by **Goben** (and purified by **Abbot Tenrin**) opens the **Cursed Realm**, where **Minamoto no Yoritomo** had bound **Musashibo Benkei** under a spell. Defeating and freeing Benkei awakens him; Benkei joyfully reunites with Saburo and joins the party. (Townsfolk also whisper that Benkei himself bore the childhood name `鬼若` — linking hero and warrior monk across generations.)

## Act 2 — The Three Imperial Regalia & Four Regions
- **Kyoto & The Emperor:** The Emperor reveals Yoritomo is trying to link **Kamakura** with **Hell** to turn Japan into a Demon Realm. Furthermore, the **Yata Mirror** and **Yasakani** at court were swapped for counterfeits, while the third treasure — the **Murakumo** — sank at **Dannoura**. Entering Yoritomo's stronghold, **Shadow Kamakura**, requires all **Three Imperial Regalia** plus the **Star Blade** sleeping on **Seven-Star Isle**.
- **Shizuka's Grave:** At Lady **Shizuka's** grave, Benkei breaks down and reveals Kijaku's parents: Lady **Shizuka** and **Minamoto no Yoshitsune**. The **Gravekeeper** bestows Shizuka's keepsake, the **Calm Flute**.
- **Iyo & Kaison:** In **Shikoku / Iyo**, they confront the Overlord of Conquest — revealed to be their old comrade **Kaison**, warped body and soul into a demon. Freed in death, Kaison dies grateful to die as a human and warns them that Yoritomo is behind everything.
- **Dannoura & Water Dragon:** Recovering the **Dragon Orb** (from **Hebizuka** / the bandits) lulls the maddened **Water Dragon** of Dannoura back to sleep, earning them the **Murakumo**.
- **Kyushu, Mt. Aso & The Dragons:** Elder **Tenzen** recounts how the land's guardian **Gold Dragon** fell, its corpse split by demonic power into the **Earth Dragon** (causing quakes beneath **Mt. Aso**) and the **Fire Dragon**. Slaying the Earth Dragon frees **Spirit Ra** to calm the earth; driving off the Fire Dragon rescues the **Mermaid Princess**, who grants the **Siren Tear**. With both dark dragons defeated, the **Gold Dragon** revives at the sacred mountain and blesses the party.
- **Forbidden Cave:** The **Dream Sage** explains to Kijaku that blood surpassing ordinary mortals becomes "demonic blood" only when bent toward evil, and bestows the **Dream Lens**.
- **Ezo, Tsugaru & Yoshitsune:** Sailing via the undersea cave from **Tsugaru Village** to **Riumo Village** in **Ezo**, the party tracks down the immortal wizard **Raphael**, who erased his memories in grief over his wife's death a century ago and recently returned believing he was "Minamoto no Yoshitsune". Using **Dante's** **Waking Fire** restores Raphael's memory; he grants the **Sky Seal** and reveals the true Yoshitsune's soul is sealed inside **Yoshitsune Mound** by the sorcerer **Gedo**.

## Climax — Hogen's Sacrifice, Shadow Kamakura & Ending
- **Saburo's Farewell:** Hogen brings O-Koto to reunite with Saburo and urges Saburo to step back and protect his daughter; Saburo bids farewell to Kijaku, Sayaka, and Benkei.
- **Hogen's Sacrifice & Yoshitsune Joins:** Confronting **Gedo** at Yoshitsune Mound / the Cursed Realm, **Hogen** expends every last spark of spiritual power — sacrificing even his future rebirth — to cast the ultimate jutsu **Jakoku-Muten** and break Gedo's curse. As Hogen passes away, Benkei weeps bitterly (`法眼どのおおおぉぉぉ!!!`). The seal breaks, freeing **Minamoto no Yoshitsune**, who embraces Kijaku as his son and joins the final party for the assault on Kamakura.
- **Shadow Kamakura:** With **Raphael** and **Seira** holding back Yoritomo's barrier, Sayaka raises the **Star Blade** at the heart of Kamakura to open the gate to **Shadow Kamakura**.
- **Final Battles:** The party defeats **Minamoto no Yoritomo**, who gasps that he never wished this and warns someone is possessing him. The true mastermind reveals himself: the **vengeful spirit of Taira no Kiyomori**, driven by the rancor of the fallen **Heike**. Encouraged by a voice of the Light (`このあんこくの世にかがやく光となれ!`), Kijaku and company destroy Kiyomori's wraith, though Kiyomori curses that humankind will tear itself apart in a coming age of war.
- **Epilogue:** Darkness is banished, though an age of warring chaos lies ahead. Saburo sets aside his famous alias and returns to Kasumi Keep with O-Koto as a righteous outlaw; Sayaka returns with Seira to Dewa Sanzan (leaving unspoken whether she truly wished it); Benkei and Yoshitsune fade into legend mourning the dead; and Kijaku lingers briefly at Rinsho-ji before setting off into parts unknown.
